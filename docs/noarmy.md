## An effort to collect events in support of the Palestinian people

As Palestinians faces one of the most brutal and prolonged assaults in modern history, people across the world are rising in resistance and solidarity.

In response, global networks are organizing flotillas to break the siege, launching street protests, holding cultural events, and building sustained campaigns to support Palestinian liberation.

A lot of groups are currently using centralized systems for sharing events like Facebook or Instagram that are unreliable, [censored](https://www.dropsitenews.com/p/leaked-data-israeli-censorship-meta), inherently centralized and often [involved in support of oppressive regimes](https://theintercept.com/2024/10/21/instagram-israel-palestine-censorship-sjp/). For movements that prioritize autonomy, horizontal collaboration, and resilience, we need tools that reflect those values.

Fortunately, there are already many communities around the world that adopt free solutions, solutions that allow this information to be easily shared, which is impossible to extract from Instagram, precisely because of the private and non-community nature of the platform.

We want to join forces with these communities to create an hub to share events in support of the Palestinian people.

- Aggregate events from across the globe
- Follow and federate with other event instances via ActivityPub (mastodon, fediverse, wordpress)
- Export events in multiple formats (RSS, ICS, embed widgets, API)

It’s a growing network of linked calendars, maintained by local groups, collectives, and solidarity organizers—each able to share their actions while also discovering others.


## How It Works

Our main instance (https://noarmy.gancio.org) can:

- Federate with other federated instances that support ActivityPub and [FEP 8a8e](https://codeberg.org/fediverse/fep/src/branch/main/fep/8a8e/fep-8a8e.md)
- Receive and display tagged events using hashtags like #freepalestine, #palestine, #noarmy, #sumud, #flotilla, #bds, #nowar
- Export a live RSS feed, so others can subscribe in readers or embed in sites
- Offer an ICS feed, so events show up directly in calendar apps
- Generate an embeddable web component, making it easy to integrate the event list into any website with minimal effort

This enables seamless integration into grassroots sites, media hubs, local organizing collectives and solidarity networks worldwide.


## What You Can Do With It

### For Organizers:

Post your events to your local event calendar using one of the following tags: #freepalestine, #palestine, #noarmy, #sumud, #flotilla, #bds, #nowar.
This will automatically share your events with the wider fediverse and all the platforms that embed the event list.
- Avoid relying on surveillance platforms

### For Websites & Media:

- Embed a live calendar showing global or regional events
- Use the RSS/ICS feeds to share updates in newsletters or social media
- Display solidarity in action, not just in words

### For Developers & Tech Collectives:

- Join the federation with your own instance
- Build tools on top of the data feeds (maps, apps, notifications)


### Who This Is For

- Free Palestine coalitions
- BDS campaigners
- Local community centers and cultural groups
- Flotilla and convoy organizers
- Palestinian diaspora communities
- Decentralized media platforms
- Anti-colonial, anti-imperialist movements around the world

If your work touches any part of the struggle for Palestinian freedom, this infrastructure is for you.
